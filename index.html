The Expensive Blind Spot

Most businesses can't see
what's costing them the most.

Revenue rarely disappears in one obvious place. It drains slowly — through the gaps between your website, your offer, your follow-up, and your operations. You feel the symptom (flat months, rising ad costs, "we're busy but not profitable") long before you can name the cause. The audit names the cause.

Broken funnels

Visitors arrive, get confused, and leave — and you never see the handoff that lost them.

Weak positioning & unclear offers

If a prospect can't tell what you do and why it's worth the price in 5 seconds, the rest doesn't matter.

Poor website conversion

You're paying for traffic that bounces against friction you've stopped noticing.

Slow follow-up & missed automation

Leads go cold in hours while manual processes quietly cap how much you can grow.

Bad customer journeys & hidden operational leaks

The work gets done, but the path is leaking time, margin, and goodwill at every seam.
