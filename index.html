Node 02 · AI Core

     

AI reads it.
Decides instantly.

     

        Every input is analyzed in real time by a language model trained to understand your context.         It scores, classifies, and routes — so the right action fires without you touching a thing.      

     
       
01Read the input content and context✓

03Classify and route to the correct workflow✓
       
04Generate personalized response or action✓
