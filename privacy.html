Privacy Policy

Effective June 1, 2026. This explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it — in plain language.

1. Who we are

Preset & Profit ("we," "us," "our") is operated by Preset & Profit, located at Virginia, United States. You can reach us at hello@presetprofit.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: the website URL you submit for an audit, your email address, and any details you choose to share (name, company, message, or context provided for a paid audit).
  • Publicly available business data: to perform an audit, we analyze the public-facing content of the website you submit. We do not request or require passwords, admin access, or private credentials.
  • Usage data: standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected via cookies or analytics to operate and improve the site.

3. How we use your information

  • To perform and deliver the audit you requested.
  • To send you your report and respond to your inquiries.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website and service.
  • To send service-related or, where you have opted in, marketing communications — which you can unsubscribe from at any time.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your audit details with third parties except the service providers necessary to operate (described below), or where required by law.

5. Service providers

We rely on third-party providers to function — for example hosting, database, authentication, payment processing, email delivery, and AI analysis. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service to us and are bound by their own obligations. Our current providers include GitHub Pages and Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database and authentication), Stripe (payment processing), Resend (email delivery), and Anthropic (AI analysis).

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate the site and, where applicable, privacy-respecting analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate business or legal purposes, then delete or anonymize it. You may request deletion at any time (see below).

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email hello@presetprofit.com and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what we collect, avoid sensitive credentials by design, and restrict access to what we hold.

10. Children

Our service is intended for businesses and is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page, and where appropriate we'll notify you.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@presetprofit.com.
